# Drasil on compact screens

At widths up to 1023px, **Menu** opens labeled workspaces and utilities. Selecting a destination closes it. Plan and Calendar each use the available width; the task tree starts closed.

Chat opens as a native modal and retains its draft and ongoing response when minimized. Forms and dialogs use the visible viewport, scrolling content, and reachable save controls. Inputs and touch controls are sized for compact layouts. Habits and My Day reserve space outside their scroll area for the chat launcher.

Weekly tasks expose **Options > Remove from this day** with confirmation and cancellation. This clears the selected day's plan and bookings while retaining the task and other-day plans.

Calendar body swipes scroll normally. Use the explicit Move control or the booking's details to change its time; touch edge-resize strips are hidden. Week view uses horizontally scrollable day columns. Month view keeps seven equal date columns. Short landscape layouts scroll rather than squeezing away the working surface.

Desktop retains the sidebar, independently resizable task/calendar panels, and expandable floating chat. Compact orientation changes retain entered form values and conversation drafts.

Browser scenarios cover narrow, tablet, and landscape layouts in Chromium and WebKit emulation. Native synthetic calendar gestures use Chromium CDP; scrolling and explicit Move are checked independently. Physical phone keyboard, browser chrome, and hardware behavior require device testing. The public showcase uses only fictional data.
